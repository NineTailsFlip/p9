Phone on 9 keys. A dialer and call screen for phones with a physical keypad and no touchscreen:
type digits to dial or to search contacts and call history, and handle calls with the green key,
the red key and the d-pad.

Features:

* One screen for dialing and call history, grouped by day
* T9 search across contacts and call history as you type
* Call screen: answer, reject or silence the ringer; mute, speaker, hold, keypad
* Swap and merge calls when a second one comes in
* Save a number from the call history as a new contact, or add it to an existing one
* Works as a plain dialer until you make it the default phone app
* An in-app manual covering every key

No internet permission, no ads, no analytics, no third-party libraries.

Part of Nine, a suite of apps for keypad phones: https://nine.furrer.life
